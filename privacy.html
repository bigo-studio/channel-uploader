Privacy Policy — Channel Uploader

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What the app does

Channel Uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos, thumbnails, captions and playlist entries to YouTube channels owned by the person running it, using the YouTube API Services.

Data it accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app receives an OAuth token allowing it to upload and manage videos on the channel you choose. It reads only the channel's name and ID (to confirm the right channel) and the status of videos it uploaded.

How data is stored and shared

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the local computer running the app. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No personal data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app has no analytics, cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from that computer.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
